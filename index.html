GOT QUESTIONS?

Good to know.

Quick answers about buying digital products.

Can I browse without creating an account?+

Yes. Anyone can browse the public catalogue and see product descriptions and prices. You need to log in to place an order.

How do I pay for a product?+

Use the payment method and account details shown at checkout. After sending the payment, submit the sender number and transaction ID. Only pay to the details displayed on the official checkout screen.

When will I receive the download link?+

Your order remains pending until the administrator verifies the transaction. Once approved, the download link appears in your order history.

Can I get a refund?+

Digital-product refund terms should be confirmed with the seller before purchase. Review the product description and contact the administrator if you have an issue.

Are my payment details verified automatically?+

No. This starter project uses manual verification by the administrator. Never share your PIN, password or one-time code.
